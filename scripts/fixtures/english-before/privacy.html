The Royal Game of Ur Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

The Royal Game of Ur for iPhone, iPad and Android. Last updated 21 October 2026.

In short: The Royal Game of Ur does not collect any data about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works completely offline.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a few things on your device only, so the game remembers them next time:

  • your settings: player names, stone colours, board design, language, sound and the shake-to-roll switch;
  • your game statistics: the results of games played on this device, shown in the Stats panel.

This information never leaves your device. Lumoxia cannot see it. You can erase the statistics at any time with “Reset all statistics” in the Stats panel, and deleting the app removes everything.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet or to any server of ours.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party software that collects data.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites and does not ask for tracking permission.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any account.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Motion sensor (shake to roll)

If you switch on “shake to roll” (it is off by default), the app reads your device’s motion sensor while the game screen is showing, only to detect a shake. The readings are used on the spot and then discarded. They are never saved or sent anywhere.

Links that open in your browser

The Rules panel has links to other websites (for example the Lumoxia pages, a history video and the credits for the sound tools) and to these pages. When you tap one, your browser opens it. That website then deals with you under its own privacy policy, which Lumoxia does not control.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated and the date above before the change ships.
