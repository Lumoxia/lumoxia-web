Common questions

How do I play?

Tap the book icon at the top left of the game for the rules. In short: roll the four dice, move one stone the number shown, land on a rosette for an extra turn, capture an opponent’s stone in the shared middle lane, and be first to bring all seven stones off the board.

Can I play against the computer, or with a friend?

Both. On the first screen choose “Single Player” to play the computer or “2 Players” to share one device.

How do I change the language, sound or boards?

The language button is at the top right. The gear icon opens sound settings, including the shake-to-roll switch. The board button on the first screen lists the eleven board designs.

How do I reset or delete my statistics?

Open Stats from the first screen. “Reset all statistics” clears everything, and you can also delete a single player.

Does the app need an account or internet connection?

No. It works offline and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
