Our Mission

Lumoxia is an indie software studio and an advocate for the AI-powered product development lifecycle. We believe the oldest games ever played still have something to teach us — and that art and modern software can carry them to a new generation.

Ancient, reimagined

We study games that are thousands of years old and rebuild them with a contemporary artistic voice — faithful to their roots, effortless to play today.

Craft over clutter

Simple gameplay, deliberate visuals, no noise. Every element earns its place, from the board markings to the sound of the dice.

AI-native by design

We build in the open with an AI-powered product development lifecycle, shipping polished software faster without sacrificing taste.

Our first app

The Royal Game of Ur

One of the oldest board games known to humanity — played in Mesopotamia some 4,500 years ago — brought to life with an artistic twist and simple, elegant gameplay.

  • Hand-crafted visuals inspired by the original British Museum board.
  • Play against a friend or a thoughtful opponent.
  • Fast to learn, satisfying to master.
